# ⚖️ Community Guidelines & Ban Policy

To keep EcoTracker Hub a helpful and safe space for everyone, we enforce the following rules. By using this platform, you agree to follow these guidelines.

---

## 📜 Core Rules

1. **Be Respectful:** No harassment, hate speech, or bullying. We are here to support each other's eco-journeys.
2. **No Spam:** Do not post ads, repetitive content, or irrelevant links.
3. **Authentic Data:** Do not intentionally input fake data to "game" the point system or badges.
4. **No Impersonation:** Do not pretend to be an Admin, Developer, or another user.
5. **Keep it Legal:** Do not share copyrighted material or engage in illegal activities.

---

## 🚫 Types of Bans

Depending on the severity of the violation, the following actions may be taken:

### 🟡 Warning (Level 1)
* **Reason:** Minor first-time offenses (e.g., light spam or off-topic posting).
* **Result:** A formal message from the Admin team. No loss of access.

### 🟠 Temporary Suspension (Level 2)
* **Duration:** 3 to 7 days.
* **Reason:** Repeated minor offenses or a single moderate violation (e.g., disrespectful behavior).
* **Result:** You can log in but cannot post or earn eco-points during this time.

### 🔴 Permanent Ban (Level 3)
* **Duration:** Indefinite.
* **Reason:** Severe violations (e.g., hate speech, hacking, fraud, or repeated suspensions).
* **Result:** Full loss of account access. Your profile and badges will be hidden from the community.

### 🧪 Shadowban
* **Reason:** Bot-like behavior or mass spamming.
* **Result:** Your posts will appear normal to you but will be invisible to everyone else.

---

## 🛡️ Appealing a Ban
If you believe a mistake was made, you can contact the **Site Administrator** at `EcoAdmin@ecotrackerhub.eu` (or your preferred contact method). Please provide your username and the reason for your appeal.
